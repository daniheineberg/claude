What are compound adjectives?

Definition

A compound adjective joins two or more words to describe a noun. We usually add hyphens before the noun so the meaning is clear.

Example: a solar-powered charger

Example Product Paragraph

My product is called GlowBottle. It is a solar-powered water bottle with a smart light in the lid. During the day, it charges in sunlight, and at night it glows so students can find it easily. GlowBottle is easy-to-use because you only fill it, close it, and place it near a window. It is also long-lasting, so families do not need to buy batteries. This product is perfect for students, campers, and athletes who need water after dark. People should buy GlowBottle because it is safe, useful, and eco-friendly. It helps users stay hydrated while saving energy. GlowBottle makes everyday water bottles brighter, smarter, and more exciting.
